---
title: Navigation
description: Bake a walkable surface from your level and make agents find their way across it.
---

Navigation lets a character find a route across your level on its own. You bake a **NavMesh**, which is a map of the ground an agent can walk on. An agent then asks for a destination and walks there, going around walls and gaps.

Quick start: [make an agent walk to a point](../09-how-to/07-make-an-agent-walk-to-a-point.md).
