---
title: Audio
description: Import sounds, play them from components and scripts, and shape them with mixers and effects.
---

Audio in Prowl plays sound files through an **AudioSource** and hears them through an **AudioListener**. Sounds can sit in 3D space and get quieter with distance. A mixer groups sounds so you can control them together.

Quick start: [play a sound](../09-how-to/04-play-a-sound.md).
