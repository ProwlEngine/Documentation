---
title: XR
description: Show your game in a headset through OpenXR, and read head and controller tracking from scripts.
---

XR means virtual reality headsets and their controllers. Prowl talks to them through **OpenXR**, the standard interface that headset makers support. The engine draws each eye, tracks the head and hands, and reads buttons and sticks.

Quick start: [Getting started with XR](01-getting-started-with-xr.md).
